Executive Summary

The problem, our solution, and the value we deliver

Enrollment in Philippine schools is still largely manual — EnrollEase moves the entire process online.

Problem

Manual enrollment causes long queues and paperwork delays for students, parents, and school registrars.

Inefficient

Solution

A web and mobile platform for online registration, document submission, and real-time status tracking.

Digital-first

Key Value

Cuts processing time by 80% — from days of queuing and manual verification to minutes online.

80% faster
Background of the Problem

Why enrollment needs to change

Four recurring pain points define the traditional enrollment experience.

Long Queues

Parents and students wait in line for hours just to submit forms and requirements.

4–8 hour waits

Slow Processing

Registrars manually verify and re-encode every document, delaying confirmations.

Manual document verification

Paper Waste

Tons of forms are printed, duplicated, filed, and stored every enrollment season.

High printing & storage costs

No Visibility

Applicants receive zero status updates and must return to campus to check progress.

Zero status updates
Proposed Solution

One platform for the entire enrollment journey

  • Online Registration

    Students and parents enroll from any device, anytime — no more lining up on campus.

  • Document Uploads

    Digital submission of requirements with secure storage and instant registrar review.

  • Real-Time Notifications

    SMS, email, and in-app alerts for every status change — submitted, verified, enrolled.

  • Data Privacy Compliance

    Built to comply with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) with consent-based data handling.

4
Aligned with

SDG 4 — Quality Education

EnrollEase supports inclusive and equitable quality education by removing the logistical barriers — queues, paperwork, and uncertainty — that keep students out of the classroom.

Business Model

Students & Parents

SaaS Subscription

Recurring monthly or annual license per school, tiered by student population and features.
